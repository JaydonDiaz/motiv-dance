About the Studio

Central Florida's First of Its Kind

Motiv Dance is Orlando's premiere all-hip-hop and street styles dance studio — and the first studio of its kind in Central Florida. Home to the Motiv Crew, as seen on NBC's World of Dance.

  • 10+ years of experience shaping Central Florida's hip-hop dance scene
  • Alumni touring with Janet Jackson, Beyoncé, Justin Bieber, Lady Gaga & more
  • 30+ world-class trainers from across the U.S. and internationally
  • Career guidance and professional networking opportunities
  • Welcoming to all skill levels — beginners to aspiring professionals

What We Offer

Drop-In Hip Hop Classes

All classes are open drop-in — no membership or contract required. Show up, check in, and dance.

01
Beginner

Foundation

Perfect for first-timers. Learn the fundamentals of hip-hop movement, rhythm, and technique in a welcoming, pressure-free environment.

  • Basic hip-hop grooves
  • Rhythm & musicality
  • Body isolation techniques
  • Confidence-building combos
02
Intermediate

Elevation

Push your skills further. Intermediate classes focus on more complex choreography, style development, and performance quality.

  • Multi-part choreography
  • Style & personality development
  • Street style vocabulary
  • Performance presence
03
Advanced

Pro Track

Train like a professional. Advanced classes are designed for aspiring commercial dancers pursuing industry-level performance skills.

  • Commercial-level choreography
  • Industry-style training
  • Networking opportunities
  • Career guidance
